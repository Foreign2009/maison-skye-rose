## Home Fragrance Composition

Home fragrance has three tiers that describe how a space unfolds olfactorily:

- **Top notes**: The opening scent impression when the product is first activated. The immediate character of the room — bright, fresh, or aromatic.
- **Heart notes**: The sustained ambient character as the fragrance settles and fills the space. The dominant accord that defines the room's identity.
- **Base notes**: The lingering ambient character that remains and anchors the space long after the initial impression. Deep, grounding, enduring.

## Rules

- Top notes: 2 to 4 specific ingredients. Opening impression — citrus, light herbs, fresh spices, light florals.
- Heart notes: 2 to 4 specific ingredients. Room-defining accord — rich florals, warm spices, soft woods, resins.
- Base notes: 2 to 4 specific ingredients. Anchoring, enduring foundation — musks, woods, amber, vanilla, resins, smoke.
- Never repeat a note across tiers.
- Never repeat a note within a tier.
- Use specific ingredient names, not categories. Write "Bergamot" not "Citrus". Write "Rose Absolute" not "Floral".
- All notes must be Title Case. "Sandalwood" not "sandalwood".
- Notes must be harmonious and appropriate for the product profile, season, and mood.
- You may incorporate or expand upon the scaffold notes provided.
- Do NOT reference: skin, wearer, application, drydown, personal projection, wearing time, or body chemistry.
