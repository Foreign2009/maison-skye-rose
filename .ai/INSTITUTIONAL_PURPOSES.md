# Institutional Purposes — Maison Skye & Rose

**Permanent record of the Institutional Purpose for each significant guest-facing page.**

Read this before any programme that touches a significant page. Evaluate every proposed change against the relevant purpose. Updated when new pages are established.

---

| Page | Institutional Purpose |
|---|---|
| Homepage | To welcome every guest into the institution and invite them to begin their confidence journey. Within thirty seconds they should understand: they are welcome, confidence is the purpose, and discovery comes before shopping. |
| About | To earn trust by honestly sharing why the institution exists, what it believes, and why it matters. Every sentence must be one the institution will still stand behind in thirty years. |
| Shop | To make discovery feel rewarding, unhurried, and guided by the guest's own curiosity — not by commercial urgency. |
| Product Detail | To build genuine confidence in the guest's choice — leaving every guest knowing more than when they arrived. Not to close a transaction. |
| Fragrance Profile | To give guests a lasting and personally meaningful record of their growing fragrance knowledge and taste. |
| Concierge | To be the most trusted and transparent recommendation experience available — explaining every suggestion, never manufacturing urgency. |
| Quiz (Scent Finder) | To help guests discover their own preferences through guided exploration — not to generate an upsell. |
| Academy | To educate genuinely — building fragrance literacy that belongs permanently to the guest, not to the institution. |
| Checkout | To reassure — communicating that the institution is grateful for the guest's trust, and that care continues after purchase. |

---

## How to Use

Before writing an implementation plan for any page above:

1. Read the Institutional Purpose for that page.
2. State it at the top of the plan.
3. Evaluate every proposed change against it.
4. If a change weakens the Institutional Purpose, do not include it.

A change that achieves its Technical Outcome but weakens the Institutional Purpose has failed.

---

## Amendment Process

This record may be extended when new significant pages are established.

Existing purposes may be refined when a programme reveals a clearer articulation.

Purposes may never be weakened to justify a commercial or technical convenience.
